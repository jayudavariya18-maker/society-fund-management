# Resident User & Operations Guide
## Bajrang Residential Society Fund Management System
### A Guide for Society Residents, Flat Owners, and Festival Organizers

---

### 1. Introduction & Equal Member Rights
Welcome to **Bajrang Society** application. This platform was built under the founding principle of **Financial Transparency & Equal Member Rights**:
- There are no separate "Admin" or "Chairman" accounts.
- Every approved flat resident can view the society accounts, deposit contributions, log expenses, generate receipts, inspect audit logs, and approve new neighbors.
- You can switch the app at any time to your preferred language: **English**, **Gujarati (ગુજરાતી)**, or **Hindi (हिन्दी)**.

---

### 2. Getting Started & Resident Login

#### 2.1 First-Time Login
1. Open the application on your phone or web browser.
2. Enter your 10-digit mobile number and tap **Send OTP**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code received on your phone.
4. If this is your first time, you will be prompted to enter:
   - Your **Full Name** (e.g., Bhavikbhai Joshi)
   - Your **Flat Number** (e.g., B-503)
   - Additional details (e.g., Owner/Tenant, possession date)
5. Tap **Submit Request**. Your application is placed into **Under Review**.

#### 2.2 Resident Peer Approval
Any existing active resident in the building can review and approve your application from their **Registration Requests** menu. Once approved, your account activates automatically.

---

### 3. Understanding Society Funds

Tap the **Funds** tab from the bottom bar (or side menu on Web) to view:
1. **General Society Maintenance Fund**:
   - Used for building operational expenses: security guards, lift AMC, water pumps, common electricity, and sanitation.
2. **Dedicated Festival Funds**:
   - Every festival (e.g., Navratri Mahotsav 2026, Diwali Snehamilan 2026) has its own independent fund.
   - Festival collections and expenses are strictly segregated and never mixed with the general maintenance balance.

---

### 4. Adding a Contribution (Maintenance / Donation)
1. Tap the green **Add Contribution** button on the Home screen or inside any Fund.
2. Select the **Fund** to credit.
3. Select the **Member / Flat** (or choose Anonymous for external donations).
4. Enter the **Amount (₹)**.
5. Select the **Payment Method** (UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer, Cheque).
6. Enter the **Transaction Reference / UTR Number**.
7. Tap **Add Contribution**.
8. The app immediately registers the transaction in the ledger and produces an **Official Society Digital Receipt** (e.g. `REC-202610-4821`).
9. You can download the receipt as a PDF or share it directly via WhatsApp.

---

### 5. Recording Society Expenses
Whenever a resident incurs an approved expenditure on behalf of the society:
1. Tap **Add Expense**.
2. Select the **Fund** to debit (General Fund or dedicated Festival Fund).
3. Select the **Expense Category** (e.g., Electricity, Cleaning, Repairs, Sound System, Food).
4. Enter the **Amount**, **Vendor Name**, and **Description**.
5. The form displays the fund's **Available Balance** in real-time.
6. Attach a photo or PDF of the vendor invoice/receipt if available.
7. Tap **Save Expense**. The expense is logged into the ledger and timestamped under your name.

---

### 6. Correcting Accounting Mistakes
If an expense was recorded with an incorrect amount or vendor:
- Society policy strictly prohibits silently erasing transactions.
- Open the transaction and tap **Make Correction / Reversal**.
- The system leaves the original record intact and records an auditable adjustment entry explaining the correction, ensuring 100% accounting transparency.

---

### 7. Managing Festivals & Events
1. Tap the **Festivals** tab.
2. Tap **Create Festival** to schedule an upcoming festival (e.g., Uttarayan 2027).
3. The system automatically creates a dedicated festival fund.
4. Add nested events (e.g., "Grand Garba Night", "Maha Aarti & Mahaprasad") with target budgets.
5. All residents can track how much was collected, spent, and remaining in real-time.

---

### 8. Financial Statements & Reports
1. Go to the **Reports** tab.
2. View the consolidated fund statement showing opening balances, total collections, total expenditures, and current net balances.
3. Tap **Export PDF** to produce a printable formal audit report.
4. Tap **Export CSV** to download a spreadsheet copy of all transactions.

---

### 9. Switching Languages
You can change the application language at any moment:
- Tap the **Language** icon in the top bar, or go to **Profile** -> **Language**.
- Select **English**, **ગુજરાતી (Gujarati)**, or **हिन्दी (Hindi)**.
- All headings, receipt templates, input forms, and error messages will switch immediately.
